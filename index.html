The Odyssey, on the hive

One story, three tellings. Homer's poem, EPIC: The Musical and Christopher Nolan's 2026 film, modelled as a Neo4j knowledge graph (130 nodes, 235 relationships) and drawn as an interactive hive plot in d3. Hover anything for the story behind it; turn on previews to hear the musical.

Building it surfaced a plot hole that weeks of reading the spreadsheet had missed. That became a piece for Nightingale, the Data Visualization Society's magazine.

The BeyHive Plot

Beyoncé's solo discography as a hive plot. Eight albums wearing their own cover art, 100+ songs coloured by era, all seven tours, and every credited collaborator with a live Wikipedia bio. Toggle previews, hover a song, and it plays.

About

The long way round.

I came to developer education through journalism, content, data and documentation, in that order. National newspapers first (The Mirror, The Week), then content and data work at Mumsnet and Finder, a stint as a data visualisation engineer at a media agency, and four years writing API documentation and doing developer advocacy at Amplience and Upsun.

In June 2026 I joined Neo4j's GraphAcademy team as a Technical Curriculum Developer. I write and film courses, contribute to the learning platform, and spend my evenings turning things I like (Homer, Beyoncé) into graphs.

Two Master's degrees, one in Data Science and one in Contemporary Literature, which explains the Odyssey. Based just outside London.
